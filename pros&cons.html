PROS AND CONS OF THE SPIRAL MODEL.

• Advantages

  1. Risk handling is the one of important advantages of the spiral model, it is the best development model to follow due to the risk analysis and risk handling at every phase.
  2. In this model, we can easily change requirements at later phases and can be incorporated accurately so this model is extremely flexible, plus, the software is developed in the early stages.
  3. It is good for large and complex projects.
  4. We can involve customers in the development of products at early phase of the software development which helps improve customer satisfaction.

• Disadvantages

  1. It is not suitable for smaller projects as it is expensive as it depends on high risk analysis and requires highly specific experts.
  2. It is much more complex compare to the other SDLC models.
  3. As the number of phases is unknown at the start of the project, so estimating the time which it finishes is difficult.
  4. Spiral may go on and on indefinitely.
